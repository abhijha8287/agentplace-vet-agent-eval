## Location & hours
- Address: 1300 S Bird Street, Boise, Idaho 83709 (Adoptions, Education & Veterinary Hospital)
- Public Veterinary Clinic hours: Monday–Friday and Sunday, 8 am – 6 pm. Closed Saturday.
- Vet clinic contact: TEXT 208-361-1799 (texts are answered faster) or CALL (208) 342-3599 and leave a voicemail.
- Adoptions & See Spot Shop: (208) 342-3508. Tue–Sat 11 am–7 pm, Sun 11 am–5 pm, closed Mon.
- Animal Care & Control / owner surrenders: 4775 Dorman Street, Boise 83705. Dispatch (208) 343-3166, daily 8 am–6 pm. After-hours animal control emergencies: police dispatch (208) 377-6790.

## Appointments & policies
- Appointments are required for exams.
- Book by TEXT: vaccine boosters, suture removal, nail trims, euthanasia drop-offs.
- Euthanasia can be scheduled by text, phone, or online request form.
- Surgery/service requests: online "Request Service" form on the website.
- Exam fees must be paid in advance.
- Payment help: ScratchPay, in-house financing, CareCredit. Financial assistance program exists for families, seniors and individuals who can't otherwise afford care.
- Wellness/comprehensive visits are being slowly reintroduced due to a veterinarian shortage (availability may be limited).
- Pets 8+ years need a pre-surgical exam (and pre-surgical bloodwork + IV catheter, priced separately / varies) before surgery.
- Emergency: the clinic is NOT an emergency hospital. Referrals: WestVet Emergency Hospital (Garden City, ID); WSU College of Veterinary Medicine (Pullman, WA) for specialty.

## Price list
### Exams
- Wellness exam $70
- Comprehensive exam $70
- Recheck exam $50
- Pre-surgical exam $70 (required for pets 8+)
- Pre-dental exam $70

### Vaccines ($20 each)
- Dog: Rabies, DA2PP, Leptospirosis, Bordetella
- Cat: Rabies, FVRCP, Feline leukemia (FeLV)

### Spay / neuter
- Dog SPAY: up to 30 lb $215; 31–75 lb $275; 76–94 lb $330; 95+ lb $375. In heat +$80. Pregnant +$95.
- Dog NEUTER: up to 30 lb $185; 31–75 lb $220; 76–94 lb $235; 95+ lb $275. Undescended testicle +$65 (inguinal) / +$95 (abdominal).
- Cat spay $95 (pregnant +$75; no in-heat add-on listed). Cat neuter $85 (undescended +$55 inguinal / +$90 abdominal).
- Brachycephalic (short-nosed) dogs: extra $100 MAY apply (Bulldogs, French Bulldogs, Boston Terriers, Pugs, Shih Tzus, Pekingese, Brussels Griffons) – often need pre-anesthesia oxygen.
- "All pets 8 years and older must have a pre-surgery exam, bloodwork, and IV catheter." (bloodwork/IV not priced)
- Microchip available Tue–Fri and Sunday; text 208-361-1799 for appointment.
- Owner-present euthanasia NOT offered. All euthanasias include communal cremation (cremains spread in mountains near Idaho City); private cremation with cremains returned = additional, unpublished fee.
- Rabbit spay/neuter: NO LONGER OFFERED
